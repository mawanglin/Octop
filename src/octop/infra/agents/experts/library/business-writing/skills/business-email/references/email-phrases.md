# 英文商务邮件常用句型

## 开头（说明目的）

- I'm writing to confirm / follow up on / request …
- Following our call on Monday, I'd like to …
- Thank you for your email regarding … Please see my answers below.
- I'm reaching out because …（陌生联系）

## 提出请求

- Could you please send me … by Friday, October 20?
- Would you be able to …?
- I'd appreciate it if you could …
- Please let me know if … works for you.

## 提供信息

- Please find the updated proposal attached.
- Here's a quick summary of where we are: …
- To answer your questions: …

## 推进下一步

- As a next step, I'll … and send it to you by …
- Could we schedule a 30-minute call next week to discuss …?
- Please confirm by … so that we can …

## 致歉

- I apologize for the delay in getting back to you.
- I'm sorry for the inconvenience this has caused. Here's what we're doing to fix it: …

## 婉拒

- Thank you for thinking of us. Unfortunately, we're not able to … at this time.
- While we can't …, we'd be happy to …（给替代方案）

## 结尾

- Thank you for your help with this.
- Looking forward to hearing from you.
- Please let me know if you have any questions.
- Best regards, / Kind regards,（正式） Best, / Thanks,（熟悉）

## 主题行示例

- Action required: Contract review by Oct 20
- Follow-up: Q4 partnership discussion
- Meeting request: Product demo next week
- Update: Delivery schedule for Order #1234
